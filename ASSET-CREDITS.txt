TerraMind AI - third-party visualization assets
Downloaded: 2026-11-14

These assets are visualization materials. They are not the scientific DEMs,
coordinate reference data, or inputs used to calculate terrain similarity.
Surface microdetail is illustrative and must not be presented as measured
Mars or Ustyurt geology. No generated UI screenshots are used as scene assets.

  public/textures/planets/earth-day.jpg

  public/textures/planets/earth-night.jpg
    Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_nightmap.jpg
    2048 x 1024; downloaded unchanged; night emission color, sRGB.

  public/textures/planets/earth-clouds.png
    Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_clouds.jpg
    2048 x 1024; JPEG converted to lossless PNG without resizing.
    Grayscale RGB cloud mask; the source has no alpha channel.
    Use as alphaMap with a white transparent material, not as an RGBA image.

  public/textures/planets/earth-normal.jpg
    Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_normal_map.tif
    2048 x 1024; TIFF converted to JPEG at quality 95 without resizing.
    Tangent-space RGB normal data; use non-color interpretation.

  public/textures/planets/earth-specular.jpg
    Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_specular_map.tif
    2048 x 1024; TIFF converted to JPEG at quality 95 without resizing.
    Grayscale specular-intensity data, not roughness or metalness.

  public/textures/planets/mars-color.jpg
    Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/8k_mars.jpg
    8192 x 4096; downloaded unchanged; planetary albedo/color, sRGB.
