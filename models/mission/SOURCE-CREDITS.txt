TERRAMIND TM-01 ROCKET CONCEPT
The 18 m TerraMind expedition rocket is an original AI-assisted, code-built
concept in src/components/mission/RocketModel.tsx, not a NASA vehicle or a
flight-qualified design. Metal, thermal-tile and lettering textures are generated
locally. Descent, exhaust, dust, takeoff and terrain flight are illustrative
animations, not a validated flight-dynamics or landing-safety calculation.
The measured DEM remains at metre scale; sub-grid material detail is illustrative.
The interactive scene carries the visible label "AI-generated".
